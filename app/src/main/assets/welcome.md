# 👋 Welcome to Orbit
Your universe of ideas, in orbit. Each planet here is something Orbit does, to try on the spot.

## 🪐 Fly around
Every idea is a planet, with its own ideas in orbit around it.
- Tap a planet
  It becomes the sun, with its ideas around it. The breadcrumbs at the top lead back.
- Spin the orbit
  Drag around the sun, or give it a flick.
- Pinch to zoom
  Zoom into a planet to go in, and out to come back.
- Go back
  Tap the sun, or swipe back as you'd leave any screen.
- See it all
  The overview button in the toolbar shows every idea at once.
- Tap 📄 to read a note
  Like this one. A dot on a planet means it has a note: fly to it, then tap 📄 in the toolbar.

## ✏️ Add ideas
Build your map right on the map.
- Tap the + in the orbit
  Type inside the new planet. Enter adds it and opens the next one, so ideas keep coming.
- Hold the + to talk
  Hold the big + and say your ideas: each pause becomes a planet. Slide up to keep listening.
- Long-press an idea
  Its menu opens where you pressed: add, rename, color, move or delete.
- Drag it somewhere else
  Long-press and move: let go on another idea to put it inside.
- Try it: add an idea here

## 🎨 Make it yours
Colors, icons and kinds of ideas, in the style you like.
- Colors
  Long-press an idea and pick a color, or any shade at all.
- ✨ Icons
  Start an idea with an emoji, or pick one from its menu.
- Kinds of ideas
  Milestones, questions, decisions, risks and people each have a look of their own.
  - Picnic on Saturday <!-- orbit type: milestone -->
  - Who brings the cake? <!-- orbit type: question -->
  - The park by the lake <!-- orbit type: decision -->
  - Rain <!-- orbit type: risk -->
  - Grandma <!-- orbit type: person -->
- Styles
  Open Style and layout, in the toolbar or the map's menu, and try Cosmos, Blueprint or Ink: this map changes as you pick.

## ✅ Plans and goals
Check off your to-dos, and the ring around their planet fills up.
- [x] Open the welcome map
- [/] Try every planet
- [ ] Check this one off
  Long-press it and set its Status to Done, or tick it in the outline.
- 💧 Glasses of water <!-- orbit type: goal, target: 8, current: 3 -->
  A goal counts up to a target. Long-press it and tap +1.

## ▶️ Present and study
Step through a map idea by idea, full screen, with each note as its caption. Or turn a branch into flashcards, and learn it.
- Take the tour
  This map has a path ready: tap ▶ at the top, then swipe, or use the volume keys, a keyboard or a clicker.
- Make a path
  Presentation path, in the map's menu: tap the ideas in the order you'll present them.
- Rehearse
  Run through it with a clock on each idea, and Orbit tells you your pace as you present.
- On a big screen
  A TV, a second screen, or Show in a browser for every phone in the room.
- 🧠 Study
  Fly into this idea and pick Study in the map's menu: recall what's under each card, then tap to see.
  - The red planet
    - Mars
  - The biggest planet
    - Jupiter
  - The planet with the brightest rings
    - Saturn

## 🗂️ Keep it or share it
No account and no cloud: every map is a plain markdown file on this device, and goes only where you send it.
- Private maps
  Turn on Private in a map's menu, and it opens only with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
- Version history
  Every map keeps its earlier versions, to look back on or bring back.
- Edit as text
  See the markdown behind a map, and change it right there.
- Send to a device
  To your other phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi, encrypted on the way.
- Export and share
  A web page that flies in any browser, a PDF, a picture, or the markdown itself.
- Keep your devices in step
  Settings can keep your maps in a folder that a sync app shares with your other devices.
