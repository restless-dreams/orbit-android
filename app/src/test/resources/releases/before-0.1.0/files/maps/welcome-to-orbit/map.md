# 👋 Welcome to Orbit
Orbit draws your ideas as a map you fly through. This map shows you around: tap a planet to start.

## 🪐 Fly around
Every idea is a planet. Tap one to fly into it, and its own ideas come around it.
- Tap a planet
  It becomes the sun, with its ideas in orbit. The breadcrumbs at the top show where you are.
- Tap the sun to go back
  Or swipe back, the way you leave any screen.
- Spin the orbit
  Drag around the sun, or flick it hard, and watch the planets turn.
- Pinch to zoom
  Zoom into a planet to enter it, and out to leave it.
- See the whole map
  The overview button at the bottom shows every idea at once.

## ✏️ Add ideas
Build the map on the map itself.
- Tap the + in the orbit
  Type right inside the new planet. Enter adds it and opens the next one, so ideas keep coming.
- Hold the + to talk
  Hold the + in the toolbar and say your ideas: each pause becomes a planet. Slide up while holding to keep listening until Done.
- Long-press any idea
  A menu where you pressed: add ideas under it, rename it, color it, move it, or delete it.
- Try it: add an idea here

## 🎨 Make it yours
- Colors and icons
  Long-press an idea and pick a color, or an emoji for its icon.
- Statuses
  To do, in progress, done and blocked. An idea with to-dos or goals under it fills a ring as they get done, and the outline adds them up.
  - [x] Read about statuses
  - [ ] Try it: check this one off
- Styles
  From Paper to Liquid glass: open Style at the bottom and watch the map change.
- Kinds of ideas
  Milestones, questions, risks, people, progress rings and goals each look their own. Long-press a goal to count it on.

## 📝 Notes and details
Every idea can carry a note in markdown, with **bold**, *italics*, lists and [links](https://hyuchia.com/orbit). Tap the note button under an idea to read it.
- Pictures and videos
  Add a photo from your phone, or a YouTube link, from an idea's editor.
- Links between ideas
  An idea can point at another one, and its button flies there.
- [Fly to the overview tips](#see-the-whole-map)

## ▶️ Present
Step through a map idea by idea, full screen, with each note as a caption. Start from the menu at the top.

## 📄 It's all markdown
Every map is a plain markdown file. Edit it as text, share it, and open it anywhere Orbit runs.
