# Without opening a map

- **A map on the home screen:**
  "Add to home screen" in a map's menu asks the launcher to place a widget for it, previewed as it will look.
  The widget shows the map as Orbit draws it, its name, and how far along its to-dos and goals are, as its outline counts them.
  A tap opens the map, and + catches an idea into it.
  - **Any size:** one cell shows the map and its name; a row puts the name and progress beside the picture, the progress's words only up to Android's Large text, where they fit, though screen readers still hear them; two rows show it all.
  - **Always current:** it follows the map as it changes, in the home screen's light or dark, and a drawing that fails doesn't stop it.
  - **In Orbit's colors:** its frame, a Home card's, rounded on every Android, its + and its bar take the app's colors, the wallpaper's while Settings has them on and Orbit's own otherwise, light or dark as the home screen is.
    The + is a finger's size, 48 dp, and screen readers hear the map's name once.
  - **Choosing its map:**
    "Choose a map" lists them, waits for them with Material's loading indicator, and without any says so, with Open Orbit.
  - **From the launcher's list:** a widget added there asks which map to show, and its settings change it.
    On Android 15 and later, the list previews the map a new widget would show.
- **Catching ideas:** a sheet over whatever is on screen, with one idea on each line, nested as the lines are indented, as pasting nests them, into the map you pick.
  A long line is named by its start and kept whole as its idea's note.
  Next time it offers the map ideas went to last.
  A map open on screen catches up at once.
  Add says "Adding…" while it adds, Ctrl+Enter adds from a keyboard, and what went wrong is announced as it shows.
  Turning the phone while it adds doesn't stop it, and ideas once in are never added twice: the sheet says so and closes.
  Drawn out of sight to add them, the map's pictures come from the map's own folder.
  A sheet left open comes back as it was when a widget's + or Quick Settings opens it again, even after Android stopped Orbit: what was typed in it stays, and a widget's + chooses its map, named above it.
  What was typed for a private map stays as private maps lock, but under a lock of its own in the sheet, never shown nor added anywhere until Unlock opens them again.
  Three ways in:
  - **A widget's +,** for its map;
  - **"Add an idea" in Quick Settings,** which waits until the phone is unlocked;
  - **"Add to an Orbit map" in another app's share sheet:** a shared list keeps its branches and its to-dos, and loses its bullets and numbers, each line becoming an idea.
    "New Orbit map" makes a map of it instead.
