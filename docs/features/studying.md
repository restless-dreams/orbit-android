# Studying

- **Flashcards from a map:**
  "Study" in a map's menu begins at the idea in focus, and on its card on Home at the top.
  Every idea under it with ideas or a note of its own is a card.
- **A card at a time:** the map flies to the card with the ideas under it hidden, their places faint in its orbit, each in its idea's color, and says what to recall.
  A tap on the map, Space or Show reveals them: they grow out of the card to their places, each place fading as its idea lands on it, and its note shows with the answers, its tables and checklists as in the details (a session checks nothing).
- **Again or Knew it:** with the buttons, the keys (1 and 2, or the arrows) or a swipe toward the answer's button.
  Each button says when the card comes back.
  Again brings it back a few cards later; Knew it sends it on to its next box: 1, 3, 7, 16, 35, 80, then 180 days.
  Undo takes the last answer back.
- **Sessions:** the cards due come first, the most overdue first, then the day's new ones, in outline order.
  Revealing a card never gives away another's: the ideas under every card wait hidden until it comes.
  A summary closes the session, with the whole branch back on the map: how many cards, how many on the first try, and when the next ones come back, with "Keep going" for new cards left for another day.
- **New cards a day:** 20 unless the learner chooses 5, 10, 30 or 50, in Settings or right where a session runs out of new cards (its summary, and "All caught up").
  Chosen there, "Keep going" brings in what the day then has room for, or another day's worth.
  Cards first studied today on any device count toward it.
- **Nothing due:**
  "All caught up" says when the next cards come back, and "Study anyway" goes through every card: knowing one early leaves it where it was, and missing one starts it over.
  With new cards still waiting, it offers "Keep going" and how many a day first.
- **Sounds:** a revealed card whose idea has a sound offers to play it, under its note: a word said aloud, for a language's cards.
  A session stops any sound playing as it starts, and each card's as the next one comes, and the small player stays out of it.
- **With private maps and drops:** a private map is studied once it's unlocked, and a session on one that locks waits behind the lock with the map, coming back where it was once unlocked.
  While a map is studied it takes no drops, and none of its ideas can be dragged out.
- **Never in the map:** how the cards are going is kept in the map's details, never its text or its history; the hidden ideas are only how the map is drawn.
  A map Orbit can't fully read is studied for the ideas it shows, and keeps the progress of the others for when it's fixed or Orbit is updated.
- **No answer lost:** each answer is kept the moment it's given, a card at a time, even as the map closes; Keep going and Study anyway go on from every answer before them, and leaving the folder keeps both this device's answers and the others'.
  When the device's storage is full, the answers wait on this device while Orbit is open, counted by the session and by the map opened again, and are saved as soon as there's room: a note under the session's bar says so, with Try again and the storage settings, and stays over the map once the session is over until they're saved.
  While a newer version of Orbit keeps the maps, this one can't save them at all: the note says so, that they go when Orbit closes, and that once Orbit is updated, the answers given then are saved.
  A later answer another device gave the same card stays, even when this one's is written after it, and Undo then gives the card back as that one left it, to go on from.
  An idea that stops being a card keeps its progress for when it's one again.
- **The map changing meanwhile:** a card removed on another device while it's studied leaves the session at once, and the next one comes; it can't be answered.
- **Renamed ideas keep their cards:** an idea renamed, on the map or in its editor, keeps how its card is going, as its latest answer on any device left it, and so does the map's center when the map is renamed from Home.
  Undo takes the card back with its old name, and redo on again.
- **Across devices, through the folder:** with the maps kept in a folder, each device's answers go there too, in its own hidden records (one per map, named after its file and kept under the device's random id, saying the ideas' ids, how each card goes, and up to eight names the map's file had before), and it reads the others'.
  A card goes as its latest answer on any device left it, so studying on the phone counts on the tablet, and two devices studying at once never clash.
  A map renamed in the folder keeps its answers on every device, and so does an idea renamed on any of them.
  Choosing a folder brings the answers along; leaving it keeps every device's on this one.
  Releases before this one never see the records; a record a newer Orbit wrote on this device, which this one would misread, or one of this device's that holds what this one can't make out, is left as it is (one a write left half done, by this version or an earlier one, is written again whole); and progress another version of Orbit wrote in a way this one doesn't expect never stops a session.
- **Everywhere:** phones and tablets, either way up (a phone on its side keeps its height for the map, with the card's panel beside it), keyboards, light and dark, English, Spanish and Portuguese.
  With a keyboard, Tab goes through the session's buttons and Space or Enter presses the one it's on, while 1, 2, the arrows and Escape still answer and stop.
  TalkBack hears how many ideas a card hides, then their names as they show.
- **From a map's card, always:**
  Study and Present on a map's card start, even on a tablet whose map is already open beside the list, whatever it was doing.
