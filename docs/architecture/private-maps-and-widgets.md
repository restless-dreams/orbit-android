# Private maps and widgets

- **Private maps are locked in Orbit, not encrypted** (`privacy/`).
  Being private is `private` in the map's `meta.json`; the map's text never says so, so files, folders and other devices see a map like any other.
  Details that can't be read count as private (see the rules for `meta.json`): such a map is locked like any other, everywhere maps are listed, backed up or imported, and its owner unlocks it and can make it not private.
  - **Covers go where `private` goes.**
    The emoji and name a private map shows instead of its own while it's locked are `cover` in the same `meta.json` (`LockedCover`, which keeps what a newer version adds to it): backups and Export all carry it with `private`, and the folder, sending to a device and shared zips leave both out.
    A changed copy Import maps brings in beside a private map here is private too, with that map's cover unless the zip gives it one of its own, as one sent from another device is (`MapBundles.importAll`, `receive`); and neither a text that matches nor the same identity ever takes a private map's versions, or files only they point at, into a map that isn't (`fillIn`).
    A private map that's locked as a zip of every map comes in is found by its identity alone (`importAll`'s `locked`, `PrivateMaps.hides`, from Settings and from `Importer`): found by its text, a zip of guessed text would tell whoever made it that the map is there, so that text comes in as a map of its own, as on a phone without it.
    A copy that stays private keeps it, saying it's a copy as its title does (`coverOfCopy`).
    Without a cover, a locked map still looks its own:
    `lockedLook` makes its color and planets from its id, never from what it holds, and `coverTitle` names it wherever "Private map" stood for its title.
  - **The prompt is Android's own,** the platform's `BiometricPrompt`, which works in any activity on Android 10 and later with no library: a fingerprint or face (`BIOMETRIC_WEAK`) or the device credential, and `setDeviceCredentialAllowed` on Android 10.
    AndroidX Biometric would need a `FragmentActivity` and bring AppCompat into the libre build.
    Orbit never sees a fingerprint, face or PIN, only whether it was the phone's owner.
  - **When they lock** is `PrivateLock`, plain state that changes at once on the main thread, so a screen coming back already shows maps locked:
    `ProcessLifecycleOwner` says when Orbit leaves and comes back, a receiver hears the screen turn off, and the setting says how long Orbit may be away.
    Orbit starts locked.
    While the phone asks, it isn't leaving: on Android 10 its PIN screen is an activity of its own.
    Without a screen lock (`KeyguardManager.isDeviceSecure`) nothing locks, since nothing could unlock it.
  - **What a lock hides:**
    Home's state leaves locked maps out of searches and tags (`homeState`), and their cards show only the lock; with `hideLockedPrivateMaps` it leaves them out of Home altogether, and Home's bar offers to show them (`PrivateMapsButton`) whether any are there or not, so the offer gives nothing away.
    That state is worked out off the main thread, a moment after the lock: its cards and search results ask the lock itself as they're drawn (`HomeCard`, `HomeScreen`'s `results`), and a new map's tag comes from the maps the lock shows as it's made (`newMapTag`).
    A paste that copies media from one map into another asks both maps' locks again once the copy is back, in the turn that sends it to the page (`MapViewModel.pasteIdeas`): a destination that locked meanwhile takes nothing, a source that did gives its text alone, and the copies (`CopiedMedia.copied`) go again.
    Every other place that lists, names or counts maps asks `PrivateView.conceals` too, and offers the same way back (`ShowPrivateMapsButton`).
    A map's screen is behind `PrivateMapGate`: opened locked, its view model and page aren't made until it's unlocked, and until the maps' summaries are read (a cold start from a shortcut or widget) whether it's private isn't known, so it counts as locked (`gate`), as it does in `MapViewModel.locked`; having been on screen counts only in this run of Orbit (`PrivateMaps.run`), so a screen Android brings back after closing Orbit starts behind the lock; locked while open, a window of its own covers the map's sheets, dialogs and pictures, which are windows too: always when Orbit was away, asking for the screen lock at once, and from Orbit's own lock (its menu, Ctrl+L) while one of them is open over the map (`WindowsOverMap`, counted by `EscapeCloses` in each, and by `CountsOverTheMap` in the menus, pickers and questions without it; `lockedWindow`), so with none open a tablet keeps its list of maps beside the lock, and an opaque cover under it keeps the first frame back from showing the map before that window draws.
    That holds only on the screen the map was open on (`openHere`): a screen made again while it's locked, as for a new language or font size, would open the map's sheets again above that window, so it shows the lock alone until it's unlocked.
    Nothing outside a map names a locked one: the folder's notes keep which of their entries are about private maps (`FolderNotes.private`, read as none from older notes), so Settings counts those (`noteEntries`); the arrival dialog leaves out the name of the private map it would update; screens whose messages or dialogs can name one close them as private maps lock (`WhenPrivateMapsLock`), and a message about a map that comes once something is done, as a deletion from Home, ideas caught into it or its being made private, is worded as private maps are as it shows, not as they were when it began (`offerBack` in `HomeScreen`, `captureInto`, `rememberMakePrivate`, and what Export all and Import maps came to, `MapBackups.Outcome.said`): one that names a private map while they're unlocked goes as they lock, even while it waits for the message before it to go (`PrivateLock.unlessLocked`), and one that names none, worded for them locked or about a map that isn't private, stays as they lock, with its Undo (`MapMessage`, `dismissAsPrivateMapsLock`): a screen hears of a lock only after it happened, so such a message can show in between; a way out that makes a file of a map and opens another app or a system screen with it (the share sheet, the save screen, the print dialog) looks at the map again right before it opens, as its details and the lock are then, not as they were when it began (`shareMapExport`, `PrivateMaps.hidesNow`): one that locked while its file was made opens nothing, and the file is deleted; and a computer the map is open on gets none of a page or zip made for it as the map locked, and hears the share ended; what takes every map out as it is, Export all and choosing the folder maps are kept in, asks for the screen lock first while a private map is locked, and always while they hide (`rememberUnlockedForEveryMap`, `asksBeforeEveryMap`); and crash reports never name any map, nor does what Android's own handler gets once Orbit's report is kept, which goes to the phone's log and the system's crash reports: the same crash, its messages as the report has them (`CrashLog.passedOn`).
    Keyboards are asked not to learn from what's typed into a private map: its fields through `KeyboardsDontLearn`, Home's rename, tags and cover for one and quick capture's ideas for one included, and the map's page through `OrbitPage.incognito`, which adds `IME_FLAG_NO_PERSONALIZED_LEARNING` to the page's input connection.
    `KeyboardsDontLearn` is there whether it asks or not, so a field under it stays as it is when what it's for changes, and the keyboard starts again with the new ask.
    Under the cover, the map's semantics are cleared and its page is invisible (`MapViewModel.locked`), so screen readers, the keyboard and drags can't reach it; the map registers no shortcuts there, and its view model opens no panel and presents, studies, undoes or redoes nothing while it's locked; its messages go as it locks, an Undo with them, and one with Undo that comes while it's locked isn't shown (`MapMessages`, cleared from the lock itself, which is heard while the activity is stopped, as Compose's state isn't); an idea or its text copied as it locks goes on no clipboard (`MapViewModel.lockedSince`); a talk going on ends as it locks, and nothing said after that joins it, however late a speech service says it (`MapViewModel.talkable`, `SpokenIdeas`), while what was said before still saves; and `ArrivalDialog` asks for the screen lock before Update writes into a locked private map.
    A map the sync adds is private from the start when it's a private map's (`MapRepository.add`'s `isPrivate`, written before the map appears): the folder's version kept beside one, a sync app's conflict copy of its file (`conflictOriginal`), or its file back after it was deleted here (the deletion's record keeps `private`); and when it may be a private map whose own file the sync can't find, by what it holds, its file renamed and changed, say (see "The folder maps are kept in").
    The folder's sync writes into a locked private map without asking, on purpose: it's the owner's own sync, running in the background, and asking would stall it until they unlocked; the map stays private and locked, and the notes don't name it.
    A private map being presented stops as it locks (`PresentationLocking`):
    `stopPresentation` sends the audiences `End` at once, even with the screen off, so browsers hear it ended and a TV or second screen goes back to waiting; a TV hears it ahead of whatever was still on its way, such as a map whose pictures were being shrunk, which then never goes (`CastAudience`), and stays connected for the next presentation.
    They close as the map's screen next draws, and until then the room's server gives out none of the map's pictures or videos, which it serves only while a map is shown, one still being made ready as the map locked included.
    Unlocked, presenting again is offered.
    Quick capture and the widget's picker keep themselves out of Recents while they name unlocked private maps, as the main window does (`keepPrivateMapsOutOfRecents`), and quick capture also while its draft is for a private map; that draft, which the sheet keeps when it's opened again, stays as private maps lock but under a lock of its own in the sheet, with Unlock, and can't be added anywhere until they're unlocked (`coveredFor`, `DraftLock`); the lock is worked out as the sheet is drawn, from private maps as they are then, so no frame of a sheet coming back shows the draft, while what's typed for a locked map shows as it's typed and as the phone turns (`shownLocked`).
  - **Recents:** screens that show something private say so while they do (`PrivateOnScreen`).
    Then Recents gets no picture:
    `setRecentsScreenshotEnabled(false)` on Android 13 and later, and `FLAG_SECURE` before, the only way there, which also stops screenshots and screen casting, so only while something private is on screen.
    A window of Orbit's own over the main one, as for a picture full screen, takes the main window's flag only as it opens, so on Android 12 and earlier it's marked itself, following what's on screen (`privateWindowPolicy`), and so is every sheet (`privateSheetProperties`): quick capture's comes up before its list names private maps.
- **Widgets are Jetpack Glance,** with a layout for each of six sizes; the launcher shows the size closest to the widget's among those that fit.
  Their pictures are the home screen's thumbnails, ready before a widget draws so it never opens on a placeholder.
  Widgets draw again once changes to the maps settle, at once as a map becomes private (`widgetRedraws`), so its widget shows the lock right away, when the home screen turns light or dark, when the text size changes, and when the wallpaper's colors are turned on or off.
  A drawing that fails (Glance's work, or its store, refusing it) is logged, never thrown, and tried again 5 seconds, 30 seconds and 2 minutes later until one takes, the next change stopping the retries with a drawing of its own; should the last fail, they're drawn as Orbit comes back on screen (`WidgetRedrawing`), so a widget never keeps a map made private by a failed drawing.
  Their colors are the app's (`widgetColors`, from `orbitColorScheme`), light and dark for the launcher to pick; before Android 12, which leaves a widget's rounding and progress bar to the launcher, the frame is a tinted rounded shape and the bar a picture of it, which says the progress's words to screen readers in a row with no room for them, as Android 12 and later's bar says its percentage.
  - **The launcher's preview** in its list of widgets (Android 15 and later) shows the map a new widget would show, never a private one.
    The launcher keeps it across Orbit's restarts and takes a new one a few times an hour, so it's offered as the maps are first read and whenever which maps are private changes, however that changed (`widgetPreviewOffers`).
    Offers go one at a time, each drawing the maps as they are once the one before has landed (`offerWidgetPreview`), so one drawn before a map was made private never lands after one drawn since.
    `widget-preview.json` in `noBackupFilesDir` says which maps the preview may show (both, while it isn't known whether the launcher took the one drawn last; not known without the file): when the launcher won't take a new one, the one it has goes if it may show a map that's private now, or if any map is private when that isn't known, and the list shows Orbit's icon until the next.
- **Launcher shortcuts follow the maps** (`ui/Shortcuts.kt`), from every change to their titles, privacy and covers, whatever made it (`matchShortcuts`, from `OrbitApplication`): each says its map's title as it is now, renamed on Home or anywhere else; a private map's says only its cover's name ("Private map" without one), however it became private (its switch also takes it out of the launcher icon's, `PrivateMaps.setPrivate`); one whose map is gone, deleted here or in the folder maps are kept in, leaves them, and one pinned is turned off, saying the map was deleted; and one pinned whose map is back, restored from the trash, is on again, as it is when the map is opened (`offerShortcut`).
  Only opening a map puts it among the launcher icon's, and only what changed is sent: Android counts each update against the few it allows in the background, and an update it refuses goes again as Orbit comes back on screen (`ShortcutMatching`), so a map made private elsewhere, synced while Orbit was away, never keeps its name on a pinned shortcut.
  Glance runs on WorkManager, whose own permissions join Orbit's in the built app:
  `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` (which reminders ask for too) to schedule the widgets' updates again after a restart, `WAKE_LOCK` so an update isn't cut short by the phone sleeping, and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` for urgent work on Android 11 and earlier (expedited work runs as a foreground service there).
  They stay: without them, widgets would stop updating after a restart, or on older phones.
  A widget added from the launcher's list asks which map it shows: its chooser (`MapWidgetConfigureActivity`) is required, as `map_widget_info.xml` has no `configuration_optional` (on Android 12 and later, launchers skip an optional one), so the launcher starts it for a result and keeps the widget only on `RESULT_OK`; one added from a map's menu gets its map from `WidgetPinnedReceiver`, and the launcher doesn't ask.
  Pixel's launcher on Android 15 and later drops a widget dragged in from its list while Android's animations are off (Remove animations, or the device scripts) before the chooser starts, any app's alike: its drop goes back to its Normal state at once, and `QuickstepLauncher.handlePendingActivityRequest` takes the request it has just made for one the person left, sending itself `RESULT_CANCELED`; the settings button it then leaves for that widget closes the launcher ("Bad widget id").
  Its Add button, the list's Add for TalkBack and a map's Add to home screen ask, or place the widget, either way, and the journeys go those ways (`WidgetJourneys`).
  An optional chooser would avoid it, and would ask no one on Android 12 and later.
