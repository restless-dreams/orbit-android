# Reminders and study mode

- **Dates remind like a calendar's** (`reminders/`).
  An idea's date is Orbit's `due` attribute, in the map's text, so every device with the map reminds of it.
  The app reads one as Orbit does (`IdeaDate`, Orbit's `parseDue`): a day, or a day and a time, in local time with no time zone, so a reminder is at that time wherever the phone is, and a date alone at 9:00.
  The editor and the long-press menu set it with Material's pickers, through the page's `update` (`due`, `null` to take it off), and the details and the outline show it, overdue once past unless the idea is done, as the planet's chip does.
  - **What reminds:**
    `Reminders` reads the maps' dates with the light reading (`MapText.dates`), never a page, and again only for a map whose text changed (`contentKey`), wherever it changed: the app follows the repository's maps (`keepRemindersCurrent`), so edits, the folder, maps sent or imported and restored versions all count.
    Done ideas don't remind; maps in the trash, or that can't be read, aren't read.
    What was read is kept in `reminders.json` beside the maps, with when each reminder was first seen and which have come or never will, by key (`handled`), so an alarm, a restart, a new time or time zone, or an update rings without reading a map (`ReminderReceiver`).
    A reminder comes once, whatever time zones the phone goes through: a date is local time, so a new zone moves its moment ahead again, or past.
    Orbit 0.5.0 kept only the moment reminders were handled up to (`handledUpTo`), which goes wrong as the zone changes: its file is read so in the zone the phone is in as this version first rings, and the moment and first sightings are still written, for 0.5.0 to go by if it's back (`ReminderBook.handledOf`).
    A reminder comes once: one whose moment passed while the phone was off comes as it starts again, but one first seen after its moment (a date set in the past, a map arriving with past dates) never does.
    Turned off in Settings, none come and no alarm is set; turned on again, only those still to come do.
  - **When:** without exact alarms, which Android 12 and later keep for alarm clocks and calendars, two alarms for the next reminder:
    `setWindow`, within ten minutes of its moment, the shortest window Android gives, and `setAndAllowWhileIdle` for a phone dozing then, which holds windowed alarms back.
    Whichever rings first shows it; the other finds it shown.
    WorkManager would wait for a dozing phone's maintenance windows, hours apart.
  - **What a notification says:** the idea and its map, and only "A reminder" on a lock screen that hides what notifications say (`setPublicVersion`).
    A private map's says only "A reminder from a private map", anywhere, whether it's locked or not, since the notification outlives the unlocking; one showing as its map is made private is said again so, in its place and without a sound, at once (`keepPrivate`, from the reading of the maps, which doesn't wait for them to settle then), 0.5.0's by their number too.
    Each reminder's notification and tap are its own, named by its key's HMAC-SHA256 (the notification's tag and the intent's identifier), never by its hash; a notification that names its idea names its map among its extras, which apps with access to notifications read, the same way, for `keepPrivate` to find it, and a private map's names none.
    The HMAC's key is this install's own, 32 random bytes in `reminder-notices.key` in `noBackupFilesDir`, made the first time and never backed up, so no other app can try likely names against what notifications carry; one that's gone is made again.
    Each notification that names its idea also carries the HMAC of `/`, which no map's id or reminder's key can be, saying which key named it: one named with a key that's gone since can't be told from another map's, so once any map is private it's said again as "A reminder", in its place and without a sound, as the private maps' are (those of the version before, which carry none, were named with the key there is).
    A tap opens the map as Orbit's own intent (`OpenMark`) with the idea (`IdeaToShow`: its name, date and place, and which of the map's ideas with that name and date it is), which the map's view model finds among the page's ideas (`ideaAmong`) and points at, as search does, once it's read; behind `PrivateMapGate`, a locked private map asks for the screen lock first, as its view model is made only then.
    Until then the idea is kept with the screens' state (`KeepIdeaToShow`), since Android may stop Orbit while the lock is asked for.
    Notifications are asked for on Android 13 and later as the first date is set, once, saying why (`NotificationsAsk`); Settings offers them again while they're off.
  - **Times on the map** follow the phone's 12- or 24-hour clock: the pages' language carries its hour cycle (`withHourCycle`, `-u-hc-h23`), which Orbit's `Intl` follows.
- **Study mode never writes the map** (`study/`).
  A session's cards are the ideas under where it began with ideas or a note under them (`cardsUnder`).
  `StudyControl` asks the page to hide the ideas under every one of them, with Orbit's `hideChildren`, so revealing one card never gives away another's, then flies to each card and reveals it with `revealChildren`: that's only how the page draws the map, so nothing is edited, saved or added to the history, and nothing reaches the folder maps are kept in or other devices.
  Each answer is kept at once in the map's `meta.json` (`MapMetadata.study`): a card's Leitner box, the day it's due and the day it was first studied, by idea id, following an idea's new id as the path does (`renameIdea`), and dropped for ideas the map no longer has as a session begins, once the map was read whole (`readOnly` is null: a map Orbit couldn't read all of, or a newer Orbit made, may show only some of its ideas, or give them other ids, and the page reports a map in the trash, or kept by a newer Orbit, in place of either).
  When new text gives an idea a new id, its card goes on under it as its latest answer on any device left it, kept as this device's own, since the other devices see only the new text, and find the card in this device's record in the folder.
  The page says an idea's new id in its `saved` (`renamed`), for text the app's editor sent (`updating`) and for text typed on the map, as the field for typing there closes on the idea (`web/renaming.ts`).
  Days are counted in the phone's time zone (`LocalDate.toEpochDay`).
  Each answer, and each one taken back, is kept as a change to that one card (`StudyChange`), written in the order they were given against this device's answers as `meta.json` has them then, under the maps' lock (`MapRepository.changeStudy`), never as a copy of them all: so one that lands after the other devices' answers were folded in (the folder was left, `keepStudyHere`, or the map came to another folder, `takeStudyElsewhere`), or after a session began again, loses nothing.
  An answer carries this device's own answer before it, which it goes over whatever the phone's clock did meanwhile; a later one there instead, another device's folded in, stays.
  They're written on the app's scope, so a map that closes still writes the ones on their way (`StudyControl.close`), and a session that begins again (Keep going, Study anyway) counts the ones not written yet.
  Each map's are in one line for the whole app (`StudyWrites`), shared by every screen of that map, so a session on a screen that opens the map again counts them too.
  A change counts as written only once `changeStudy` wrote it: one the storage can't take, or that a newer Orbit keeping the maps refuses (`updateMetadata`'s `strict`, which only it uses), stays first in line, the ones after it waiting behind so an answer taken back never lands before the answer, and is tried again every 15 seconds, at once on Try again, and as another answer comes (`StudyWriting`), while Orbit runs.
  They're only in memory, as a map's unsaved text is: no file of their own, which a full disk couldn't take either and every later version would have to read; `MapUi.answersUnsaved` says why they wait, and the folder maps are kept in is asked to sync only once they're written.
  Undo gives the card back as this device's own answer before it, or, once the other devices' answers were folded into this device's, as they all left it; an answer the folder brought in later stays, and the session shows the card as the details will have it then, so it goes on from that one.
  A card the map no longer has as a card, as it changed elsewhere while it's studied (a session takes no edits), leaves the session at once and takes no answer (`Study.without`).
  A box under the first or past the last, as another version may write, counts as the nearest, and stays as it was written until the card is answered (`answered`).
  Home counts the cards due among the map's cards only: as the map's page reads it whole, the ideas with answers that aren't cards (gone, or with no ideas or note under them) are noted in `meta.json` (`studyNotCards`, by `noteCards`), and their answers stay for when they're cards again; Home's day moves on at midnight, as the phone's date, time or time zone changes, and as Orbit comes back on screen (`rememberStudyDay`).
  A page that loads the map again (its process stopped, say) gets the hidden ideas and the card back (`pageLoaded`).
  Behind a private map's lock (`LocalMapLocked`, from `PrivateMapGate`), a session holds still: it takes no keys or focus, keeps the screen on no longer and says nothing to TalkBack, and it's just where it was once unlocked.
  A studied map takes no drops (`takesDrops`), and its ideas don't drag out.
