Keep maps private, locked with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Study a map as flashcards: its ideas stay hidden until you reveal them, and each card comes back when it's due. Record a voice memo on an idea, or add a sound. Live photos play their motion, and GIFs move. Drag text, pictures and maps in from other apps, and ideas out. Version history says when, why and what changed. Sharing says what goes with a map, and pictures leave without where they were taken.
